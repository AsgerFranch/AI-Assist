Google Gemini API Configuration
The Render Workflow Assistant uses Google AI Studio Free Tier to analyze your images and synthesize prompts locally. Your key is stored strictly in your browser's localStorage.
Don't have a key? Get a free API key at Google AI Studio (takes ~15 seconds).
How to Export to Gemini
The Workflow:
1. Paste your screenshot and click Synthesize.
2. Click Copy Image and paste it (Ctrl+V) into the Gemini chat box.
3. Click Copy Prompt and paste it right next to the image in Gemini.
4. Hit send in Gemini!
Why two steps instead of copying both at once?
Web browsers have strict security limits that prevent copying rich media (images and text combined) directly to your clipboard in a way that all web apps understand perfectly. Gemini's input field strongly prefers receiving the image and text separated. Copying them individually ensures Gemini reads both elements perfectly every time without glitching.
